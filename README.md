## 🚀 Overview
NEXUS_AI is a concept landing page designed with a "Metro/Cyber" aesthetic. It utilizes advanced scroll mechanics and 3D visualizations to tell the story of enterprise AI integration.

### Key Features
*   **3D Sensorium:** Interactive Three.js background with floating geometric wireframes (Icosahedron, Torus, Octahedron) and particle grids.
*   **Scroll-Driven Animation:** Advanced GSAP integration linking scroll position to 3D camera movement and object mutation.
*   **Multimodal Horizontal Scroll:** A unique "pin-and-scroll" section visualizing Vision, Audio, and Lidar processing.
*   **Agent Swarm:** A finale visualization where all 3D assets converge into a complex orbiting system.
*   **Cyber Aesthetic:** Neon accents (`#0aff0a`, `#00f3ff`), glassmorphism, scanlines, and glitch text effects.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
*   **Core:** HTML5, CSS3 (Custom Properties & Flexbox/Grid)
*   **3D Engine:** [Three.js](https://threejs.org/)
*   **Animation:** [GSAP](https://greensock.com/gsap/) + ScrollTrigger
